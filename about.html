About us

Useful technology learning, without the jargon.

NextGen Tech Guide is an independent learning blog focused on clear explanations and practical guidance for people exploring IT concepts and platforms.

What you’ll find here

We publish beginner-friendly explainers, study roadmaps, and checklists that help readers understand a topic before deciding how to learn it in more depth. ServiceNow is one of our starting topics, alongside broader IT fundamentals and career skills.

Our approach

Our goal is to be useful, clear, and realistic. We aim to distinguish general learning advice from details that readers should confirm with an official provider or product source. Articles are educational and are not a guarantee of certification, employment, or a particular career outcome.
